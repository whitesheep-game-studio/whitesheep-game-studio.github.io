# Japanese landing assets

- `share.png`: 1200×630 social card composed from the studio palette and the existing
  app icons in this repository. No external artwork or remote font requests.
- The three SVG QR codes are generated by `scripts/generate-japanese-qr.py` using
  qrcode 8.2 (BSD license). Each filename is the first 16 hex characters of the
  SHA-256 of the exact destination in `apps.json`. No redirect or tracking service.
- `../badges/app-store.svg`: existing official Apple artwork, reused unchanged.
- `../badges/google-play-official.png`: Google's official English badge, downloaded
  unchanged from https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png
  on 2026-11-27. Guidelines: https://developer.android.com/distribute/marketing-tools/brand-guidelines
  The PNG includes clear space; CSS matches visible heights without editing artwork.
  Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC.
